## Convenience improvements

- Skip the initial slideshow interval when starting on an unpublished article.

# 0.1.0
## Main features
- Browse graphical abstracts and article images from ACS, Nature, Cell Press, RSC, Wiley, and Science journal listings.
- Scan articles in a slideshow or navigate by touch or keyboard, and resume from a saved reading position.
- Filter by article type or keyword and highlight keyword matches.
- Open or share HTML and PDF article links and continue through a configurable sequence of journal listings.
